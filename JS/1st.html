VELLORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

SPORTS MEETUP

NOTICE

The annual sports meetup for Vellore Institute of Technology, a prominent running event drawing elite athletes and fitness enthusiasts alike, will take place on March 24, 2026, 8:00AM. This prestigious race will start at AB-1 Portigo, VIT Chennai, renowned for its scenic pathways and vibrant atmosphere. Participants will navigate a 26.2-mile route through iconic landmarks, including the MGR Circle, ensuring a memorable experience. Spectators can enjoy live music performances and food vendors along the route, creating an engaging environment for all attendees. Registration details and more information are available on the event’s official website. Students who are interested in participating in any or all of the events are requested to contact their respective proctors and submit their names by the 20th of March. This is also to inform you that Mr. G Vishwanathan, Chancellor of VIT Chennai, has been invited to the sports day event as a chief guest. All the students are expected to participate enthusiastically. For more details, please contact the undersigned.
